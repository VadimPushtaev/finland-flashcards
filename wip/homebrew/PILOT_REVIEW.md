## What is delivered

- 15 sections, approximately 6,800 words, 194 verbatim text units and 470 sentence
  ranges, from 18 archived files across all three content sources.
- Fundamental and human rights; equality and discrimination; remedies and racism;
  language/minority rights and accessibility; children and religion; sexual and
  reproductive autonomy; individual duties, citizenship and defence.
- Whole explanations, examples, exceptions and list items. No arbitrary length
  cap. Some overlap remains where a general right is followed by an application.
- Stable chapter, heading, block, item and sentence IDs. Exact source locations,
  attribution and editorial decisions remain in separate English metadata.
- No translated content, practice questions, remote assets or exam logistics.

The rights-law overview supplies the main explanation. InfoFinland adds useful
qualifications and concrete protections. Two textbook paragraphs add the
constitution's status/commencement and the constitutional roots of equality law.

## Matters to review

1. **Reading depth:** the draft deliberately keeps examples, disability rights,
   children's rights and the full reproductive-rights conditions. Some of this
   material will also inform chapters 07–10; later chapters should avoid repeating
   these explanations wholesale.
2. **One recorded gap:** the rights lists use blanket extradition wording. Retain
   the entry/non-expulsion clauses verbatim as a list item; hold only the separate
   extradition claim until a suitable local passage is identified. The gap is explicit
   in metadata.
3. **Archived wording:** service ages, authority names, care thresholds and source
   statistics are not a verification of 2026 law. Clearly dated 2023/2024 examples
   remain dated. Source disagreements resolved here use only local evidence.
4. **Source defects:** a few spelling/grammar mistakes remain, including “ssådant”,
   “Nät”, “kan du kan du” and a malformed Sami-rights phrase. The agreed filtering
   approach preserves these rather than silently rewriting the sources.
5. **Schema scope:** paragraph/list production is implemented; table/image shapes
   are specified and tested, but their real source extraction and rendering must
   be added when a later chapter actually needs them.
