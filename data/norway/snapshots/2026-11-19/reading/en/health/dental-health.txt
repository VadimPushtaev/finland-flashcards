Dental health
https://samfunnskunnskap.no/en/health/dental-health.html

Children and young people have regular dental check-ups at the public dental service until they turn 19. These check-ups are paid for by the public authorities. Young adults pay 25 per cent of the cost of dental treatment in the public dental service, while the public authorities pay the remaining 75 per cent. Adults otherwise pay full price for dental treatment. Adults must make their own appointments with a private dentist. It is common to have a check-up once a year. If a child or young person needs dental braces, their parents have to pay a patient charge.

Children’s dental health

Parents are responsible for their children’s dental health. Sugar is bad for the teeth. A lot of the things we eat and drink contains sugar, e.g. chocolate, biscuits, fizzy drinks, squash, cakes and sweets. Everyone should brush their teeth twice a day, morning and evening. Poor milk teeth may mean that a child’s new teeth will also be poor. It is therefore important to look after children’s teeth from an early age.
