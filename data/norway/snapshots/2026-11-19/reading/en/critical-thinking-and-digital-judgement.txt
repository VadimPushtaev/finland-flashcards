Critical thinking and digital judgement
https://samfunnskunnskap.no/en/critical-thinking-and-digital-judgement.html

Discuss the term ‘ethics’. What does data protection mean? What do you think is meant by critical thinking?

Digital media and digital social platforms have a lot of influence today. How we deal with online information and communication has become important. It’s easy to find information and facts, but it’s also important to ask critical questions about what we read.

We make many decisions every day. Our ethical awareness helps us to make the right decisions for society, for the people around us and for ourselves.
