The right to a free and independent life
https://samfunnskunnskap.no/en/the-right-to-a-free-and-independent-life.html

What does living a free and independent life mean? Can people make their own independent decisions without taking other people’s wishes and opinions into consideration? Who influence our decisions?

An important value in Norwegian society is that everyone has the right and freedom to make independent choices. Although we live free lives with the opportunity to decide for ourselves, there will always be people around us who we listen to and take into consideration. Everyone has the right to live a free and independent life with the support and love of those closest to them, and a life without violence and threats.
