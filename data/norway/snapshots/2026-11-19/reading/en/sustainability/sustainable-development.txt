The UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a common plan for sustainable development in the whole world. Their goal is to eradicate poverty, combat different types of inequality and stop climate change by 2030.

Climate change

The weather and the climate are not the same thing. The weather is what we see every day when we are outside or look out of the window, while the climate is the average weather measured over a long period. Over the past 100 years, major changes have taken place in the earth’s climate. Most of the world’s climate researchers believe that human CO2 emissions are behind the rapid and far-reaching changes we see today.

Renewable resources

Renewable resources is a term that describes self-renewing natural resources. People can draw on these resources, and if exploited properly, they are not used up.

Locally produced food

Locally produced food is produced near the consumer When food is produced locally, there is less need for transport and we thus save a lot of greenhouse gas emissions. It is also easier to control the production of locally produced food. This may concern crop sprays, the use of antibiotics in the meat and fish industry and animal welfare conditions.
