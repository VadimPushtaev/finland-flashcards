What is a family? What similarities and differences are there in family life and bringing up children in different cultures you know of? How does moving to a different country affect family life? Can this be perceived differently by different members of a family?

Living together in a family is nice, but it can also be challenging at times. When a family moves to a new country, challenges may also arise that they haven’t thought about in advance. Children and adults meet a new language and culture at school and at work. At the same time they may really miss the people and places they have left behind. The different members of a family may react differently to this. It is important to support each other, talk together and find ways of moving on.
