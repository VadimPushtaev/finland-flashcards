SOCIAL STUDIES
https://samfunnskunnskap.no/en/index.html

You will find the subject matter for the curriculum for ‘Social studies for adult immigrants’ (2021) here. Read the texts and work on the tasks. It’s also a good idea to discuss them with others. This resource covers the subject matter you have to learn to prepare for the test in social studies (75 hours) and the Norwegian Citizenship Test. For the teacher: Teaching tips and additional resources are available under each topic.
