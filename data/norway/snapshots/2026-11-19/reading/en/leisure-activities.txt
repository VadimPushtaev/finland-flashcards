What do you associate with spare time? How do people usually spend their spare time in countries you are familiar with? Can you do the same things in your spare time here in Norway as you used to be able to? What do you like to do in your spare time? Do men and women have the same amount of spare time?

Spare time is the part of the day that we do not spend working, eating or sleeping or doing other designated duties. Spare time can be said to be the time we decide over ourselves, more or less. It’s not always easy to set a clear boundary between spare time and work or between spare time and household chores.
