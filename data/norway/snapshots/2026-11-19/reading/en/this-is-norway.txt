What is the population like in your country of origin? Does it comprise different ethnic groups? Does the country have national minorities? Are many different languages spoken? How do people usually live together? What do you know about the population of Norway?

The population of Norway is growing. This growth is due to immigration and that more people are born than die. In 1970, the population was just under 3.9 million. In 2025, there are now more than 5.6 million people living in Norway. The country's population is expected to reach 6 million by the year 2040.

Although Norway’s population is complex, numerous things nonetheless unite us as a nation. Values, such as equality pervade society, most people have a relatively high standard of living, and the population has a relatively high level of education.

There is full freedom of religion in Norway. Many people are Christians, and the law is based on Christian and humanist values.
