Political engagement
https://samfunnskunnskap.no/en/leisure-activities/political-engagement.html

Everyone who lives in a democracy is responsible for ensuring democracy works. It is important that people are active and keep up-to-date with what is happening. It is also important that everyone is entitled to their opinions, and that they express them.

Young Norwegians are more politically active than they used to be. Election turnout among the youngest voters increased by more than ten per cent from 2007 to 2011 and has remained high ever since. Membership of political youth organisations has also increased. Membership of different organisations provides knowledge of and practice in different democratic processes, such as discussions, board work and elections and votes.
