Personal finances
https://samfunnskunnskap.no/en/personal-finances.html

Why is it important to have good control of your finances? What do you spend most money on? What factors impact your consumption? What does it take to have a good quality of life?

Most adults receive pay, social security or pension once a month. Many people draw up a budget. A budget is a plan for how this money is to be spent.

Just over 35 per cent of Norwegian households’ budget is spent on housing expenses, while the average household spends around 11 per cent of their income on food (Statistics Norway, 2022). Our consumption is affected by many factors. For example, how big your family is, what kind of activities the family members want to do or quite simply what is perceived as the norm in your network.
