What do you associate with the word democracy? What fundamental rights do you think a person should have?

Norway is a democracy. This word comes from ancient Greek and means ‘rule by the people’. The people decide what policies Norway will pursue by electing representatives of political parties to the Storting and to county and municipal councils. This means that the majority of the people decide. However, it is also an important principle that the majority must take the opinions of the minority into consideration in policy decisions.

Rules of democracy and human rights complement and reinforce each other.
