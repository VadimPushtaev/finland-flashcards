Yhteiskuntaorientaatio.fi

Multilingual learning materials for the early stages of integration

Yhteiskuntaorientaatio.fi is a learning materials website designed for instructors, supporting the implementation of multilingual civic orientation. The site provides information about living, everyday life and working in Finland, as well as a teacher’s guide developed to support instruction.

Multilingual civic orientation is a statutory service in the early stages of integration. The site’s content and structure are based on the government decree concerning civic orientation under the Act on the Promotion of Immigrant Integration.
