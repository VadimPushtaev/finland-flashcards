## Words and names

Check **kiuas**, **löyly**, **vihta**, and **lauteet** in sauna culture; the two
bounded Finnish-word comparisons were inconclusive and the originals were retained.
Other useful listening priorities are **tu man hand**, **naturreligion**,
**livsåskådningskunskap**, **Kristi himmelsfärdsdag**, **Runeberg**, **Minna Canth**,
**nowruz**, and **Ateneum/EMMA**. Life celebrations also has uncertain phrase
boundaries around *Efter lägret hålls en fest hemma*, *kaffebjudning*, and
*På kalasen leker man*.

Resolved dates, country-abbreviation expansions, and selected compound-word retakes
are documented in the detailed notes and are not presented as unresolved dates.
An ASR spelling such as `4` for *firas* alone does not establish a spoken number.
