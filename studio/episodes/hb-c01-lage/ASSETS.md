The [accepted video](../../../videos/homebrew/hb-c01-lage.mp4) includes narration,
music and final layout. Temporary audition audio and review previews were removed.
Source media and maps are local caches restored by the shared asset tools.

The [asset register](assets.yaml) records exact files, hashes, creators, license
links and relevance checks. [Voice comparison data](tts-comparison.json) retains
all seven voice configurations (five Supertonic presets, Alma, OmniVoice), their
actual inputs, measured durations and raw local-ASR transcripts. The shared
[voice instructions](../../TTS.md) explain the selection and its limits.

The music file is the official catalog download for ISRC USUAN1200006 and has the
expected 162.48-second duration; its embedded ID3 title says “var 3”. Preserve
that discrepancy in provenance; credits follow the publisher's catalog title.

[Per-sentence diagnostic notes](narration-check.json) retain the original ASR
flags for focused review on future revisions. These are unconfirmed discrepancies,
not findings of pronunciation errors; the user accepted the complete video.

Background revision: the existing Kuivaketvele original now supplies the full-frame
decorative layer throughout the video. Its darkening, blur and continuous pan/zoom
are recorded in the script and asset register. No new downloads or narration
generation were needed. Maps and the Saimaa photograph remain useful foreground
assets; the first two sentences intentionally have no foreground image.
